# StackTrack Credit Economy Benchmarks

## Currency Overview
Primary currency: Credits 🟠

Credits should be the core in-game economy unit used for progression, purchases, and reward pacing.

## Item Target Costs
- Basic furniture: 250–1,000 credits
- Common clothing: 500–1,500 credits
- Emotes: 750–2,000 credits
- Tournament entry (casual): 100–250 credits
- Tournament entry (competitive): 500–2,000 credits
- Premium room theme: 5,000–20,000 credits
- Rare cosmetic: 10,000–50,000 credits

## Reward Sources
### Card Scanning
- New card added: 10–25 credits
- Rare card: +100 to +1,000 credits
- Collection milestone: bonus rewards

### Practice Matches
- Rookie win: 100 credits
- Collector win: 250 credits
- Pro win: 600 credits
- Elite win: 1,500 credits

### Tournament Rewards
- Scaled based on entry fee and placement.

### Daily Login
- Starts small and escalates to a weekly jackpot.

### Achievements
- One-time bonuses for meaningful milestones.

## Tournament Payout Model
Example: 100-credit entry tournament
- 100 players × 100 credits = 10,000 credits collected
- 70% (7,000) paid out as prizes
- 20% (2,000) reserved for sinks/events
- 10% (1,000) platform margin

Suggested distribution:
- 1st: 2,500 credits
- 2nd: 1,500 credits
- 3rd: 1,000 credits
- 4th–10th: 250 credits each

## Daily Login Rewards
- Day 1: 100 credits
- Day 2: 150 credits
- Day 3: 250 credits
- Day 4: 350 credits
- Day 5: 500 credits
- Day 6: 750 credits
- Day 7: 2,000 credits + chest

Weekly total: 4,100 credits.

## Achievement Rewards
- First card scanned: 250 credits
- First match win: 500 credits
- Defeat first rival: 500 credits
- 10 cards scanned: 1,000 credits
- Win 10 tournaments: 10,000 credits

## Credit Sinks (Essential)
Without sinks, inflation ruins the economy.

Primary sinks:
- Tournament entries
- Cosmetic purchases
- Room upgrades
- Limited-time offers
- Seasonal battle passes

Note: watching tournaments and NPC practice are intentionally free activities to keep onboarding low-friction.

Aim for players to spend 70–90% of what they earn.

## Earnings Targets
### Casual Player (15–20 min/day)
- Daily rewards: 100–500 credits
- Practice wins: 200–600 credits
- Scanning: 50–200 credits

Target earnings: 500–1,500 credits/day

### Engaged Player (1+ hr/day)
- Tournaments
- Streak bonuses
- Achievements

Target earnings: 2,000–10,000 credits/day

## Shop Pricing Tiers
- Common: 250–1,500 credits
- Rare: 5,000–15,000 credits
- Epic: 20,000–50,000 credits
- Legendary: 75,000–250,000 credits

## Time-to-Earn Targets
- Small item: 1 session
- Rare item: 3–7 days
- Epic item: 2–4 weeks
- Legendary item: 1–3 months

## Streak Bonuses
Daily activity streak:
- +10% rewards after 3 days
- +25% after 7 days
- +50% cap after 30 days

Tournament win streak:
- Incremental bonuses up to +100%

## Battle Pass Model (Optional)
Free and premium tracks.

Typical rewards:
- Credits
- Cosmetics
- Room items
- Exclusive animations

Premium price could be set in Gems or real currency.

## Inflation Controls
Monitor:
- Average credits earned/day
- Average credits spent/day
- Median balances
- High-balance concentration

Target:
- Spend ratio ≥ 80% of credits earned

## Starter Economy (First 7 Days)
During onboarding and early retention, be generous.

Day 1–7 total rewards:
- 10,000–20,000 credits
- Several cosmetics
- Multiple room upgrades

This creates rapid early progress.

## Example Player Journey
### Day 1
- Onboarding rewards: 1,500+ credits
- Daily reward: 100 credits
- First win rewards: 1,000+ credits

Total: ~3,000 credits

### Week 1
- Daily rewards and achievements
- Several practice wins

Total earnings: ~10,000–20,000 credits

### Month 1
- Can afford rare cosmetics and premium room items

## Seasonal Economy
Each season introduces:
- New cosmetics
- New rivals
- Exclusive tournaments
- Limited-time currency sinks

This keeps long-term players engaged.

## Recommended Launch Numbers
### Earnings
- Casual players: 500–1,500 credits/day
- Engaged players: 2,000–10,000 credits/day

### Costs
- Tournament entries: 100–2,000 credits
- Common cosmetics: 250–1,500 credits
- Premium cosmetics: 20,000+ credits

### Savings Targets
- Rare item: 3–7 days
- Epic item: 2–4 weeks
- Legendary item: 1–3 months
